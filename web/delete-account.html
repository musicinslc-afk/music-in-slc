Music in SLC, website and app

You can delete your Music in SLC account here. It happens right away and can't be undone.

What gets deleted

  • Your email address, display name, handle and visibility setting
  • Your Maybe and Going shows and your friend connections
  • Messages and requests you sent through the Submit page with the same email
  • Your name, email and submission notes on shows sent from that email

What stays

  • Show listings you submitted, without your personal details
  • Google Analytics statistics already collected, including Maybe and Going activity. Deleting your account doesn't remove them.
  • Email already in the musicinslc@gmail.com inbox. Ask us and we'll delete it within 30 days.
