Privacy

Effective November 27, 2026

Music in SLC is a free calendar of live music in Salt Lake County, on the web at musicinslc.com and as an app. This page explains what it collects, why, and how to remove it. The short version: browsing needs no account, there are no ads, nothing is sold, and the only tracking is Google Analytics, which counts visits so we can see what's working.

Without an account

You can browse every show without signing in. Your filters, your Maybe and Going picks, and any taste you set up (artists you type in or a Last.fm username) are saved only on your device, in your browser's storage. They never reach our servers unless you sign in. Clearing the site's data in your browser, or uninstalling the app, removes them.

If you connect Last.fm, your device asks Last.fm for your public listening history and similar artists directly. We don't receive it. Last.fm's own privacy policy covers those requests.

Visit statistics

The site and app use Google Analytics to count visits: which pages are viewed, roughly where visitors are (city level), what kind of device and browser they use, how they arrived, and which buttons they use, such as Tickets, Maybe and Going. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to tell repeat visits apart. It does not receive your name, email, plans or listening history, and Google signals and ad personalization are turned off, so this data is not used for advertising. Google's privacy policy covers how it handles the data. You can block it with a browser's tracking protection or Google's opt-out add-on, and the site works the same.

With an account

Signing in is optional. It lets your plans follow you between devices and lets friends see where you're going. When you sign in we store:

  • Your email address, used only to send your sign-in link
  • Your display name and handle
  • The shows you mark Maybe or Going
  • The people you add as friends
  • Who can see your plans: just you, friends, or everyone

Other people see your name, handle and plans only as your visibility setting allows. Show totals count everyone without naming anyone.

Submitting shows, venues and questions

When you submit a show, we keep the show details along with your name and email. Your email is used only if we have a question about the listing and is never shown on the site. If you send a list of shows, a venue, a special event or a question, we keep what you send, your name, email and whether you are an artist, venue, promoter or fan. Only site admins can read it. Shows emailed to musicinslc@gmail.com are read to add them to the calendar.

Who processes the data

  • Supabase hosts the database and sends sign-in emails.
  • GitHub Pages serves the website and app files.
  • Google Analytics counts visits and page views.
  • Anthropic reads emailed flyers and show announcements to turn them into listings. Account data is not sent to it.

Data is sent over encrypted connections. We don't sell or share personal data with anyone else, and we don't use it for advertising.

Delete your account

Email musicinslc@gmail.com from the address you sign in with and ask to delete your account. Within 30 days we delete your email, name, handle, plans and friend connections. Shows you submitted stay on the calendar, but your name and email are removed from them. Messages and other requests you sent are deleted. You can also email to ask what we hold about you or to correct it.

Children

Music in SLC is meant for a general audience and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
